## Hello, I'm Miroslav

Shortly, I'm:

- former professional developer,
- open-source enthusiast and creator of [ArchiveKeep](https://github.com/archivekeep/archivekeep) application,
- manual labourer.

### About

I love open-source. I'm building the [ArchiveKeep](https://github.com/archivekeep/archivekeep) — it helps you keep your data safe across multiple storages, without lock-in.

I believe technology should serve people, not the other way around. Tools should let you own your things — your data, your files, your freedom — instead of locking you into a company that becomes your single point of failure.

I am not fond of technologies locked to individual giants. Companies can change rules anytime, whether you like it or not — and with vendor lock-in, they become a single point of abuse: forced terms-of-service changes and anti-features creep into systems you can't escape.

I stepped away from the career track for reasons that might be best left unsaid, or maybe I'll share someday. Now I enjoy manual labor and do open-source in my free time.
